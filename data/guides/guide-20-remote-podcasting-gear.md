# Best Gear for Remote Podcasting in South Africa

> Start a podcast with remote guests. The best budget microphones, headphones, and hosting platforms for South African podcasters.

Podcasting in South Africa is having its moment. Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube are aggressively pushing local audio content, and the barrier to entry has never been lower. But here is the truth most "start a podcast" guides miss: the hardest part is not the recording. It is recording guests who are not in the same room as you.

This guide covers the exact gear and tools you need to launch a professional-sounding remote podcast from South Africa — without spending a fortune.

---

## Why Remote Podcasting Is the Default in 2026

Even post-pandemic, most SA podcasts are recorded remotely. Here is why:

- **Guest access.** You can interview anyone in the world.
- **Lower cost.** No studio rental, no travel.
- **Scheduling ease.** Easier to book busy guests for a 60-minute Zoom call.
- **Quality is now indistinguishable** with the right gear.

The catch: remote recording demands more from your setup. You need a mic that flatters your voice over a digital connection, closed-back headphones so guests do not hear themselves echo back, and a stable internet connection.

## Essential Accessories

- **Boom arm** — saves desk space and lets you position the mic correctly.
- **Pop filter** — eliminates plosives (the harsh "p" sounds).
- **Shock mount** — prevents desk vibrations reaching the mic.
- **Acoustic foam** — tames room echo if you record in a hard-surface room.

You can buy most of these on [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H99MHP7T?tag=freedomwheels-20) as part of a podcast bundle.

## Remote Recording Tools (Software)

Hardware is half the battle. The right recording software matters just as much.

### Riverside.fm

The gold standard for remote podcast recording. Records each guest locally in 4K video and uncompressed audio, then uploads to the cloud. Free plan covers 2 hours/month.

### Squadcast

Similar to Riverside with slightly better multi-guest handling. Paid plans start around $20/month.

### Zencastr

Browser-based, records each guest locally, free for up to 8 hours/month. A good starting point.

### Cleanfeed

Free for single-track recordings. Excellent for radio-style interviews.

## Common Mistakes SA Podcasters Make

1. **Skimping on the mic.** Your phone mic is not good enough. Ever.
2. **Recording in echoey rooms.** Soft furnishings (curtains, rugs) absorb reverb.
3. **No headphones.** Guest audio bleeds into your mic and creates feedback.
4. **Skipping the website.** Spotify links expire. Your Hostinger site is permanent.
5. **Boring cover art.** Spend 2 hours in Canva — it is worth more than 50 hours of audio.
