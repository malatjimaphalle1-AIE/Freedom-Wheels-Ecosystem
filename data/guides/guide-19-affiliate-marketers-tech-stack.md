## What Is Affiliate Marketing (Briefly)

Affiliate marketing is performance-based marketing. You promote a product, someone buys through your unique link, you earn a commission.

The biggest affiliate networks accessible to SA marketers:

- **Amazon Associates** — global, physical products, 1–10% commission.
- **Hostinger Affiliates** — hosting, high payouts (often 60% of sale).
- **Software SaaS programs** — recurring commissions, ideal for niche sites.
- **Local programs** — Takealot, Zando, Travelstart.

This guide focuses on the Hostinger + Amazon Associates stack because it is the most accessible, lowest-friction entry point for a South African.

## How to Pick a Niche

Your niche determines your success. Pick wrong and no amount of SEO will save you.

### Good niche criteria

1. **Commercial intent** — people search to buy, not just learn.
2. **Product diversity** — at least 50+ products to promote.
3. **Manageable competition** — avoid "best laptops" (too broad) but also avoid "best left-handed scissors under R200" (too narrow).
4. **Personal interest** — you will write 100+ articles. Pick something you care about.

### Examples of strong SA-friendly niches

- Coffee gear for SA homes.
- Off-grid / load-shedding solar equipment.
- Hiking and camping gear for SA trails.
- Budget home office setups (very on-trend in 2026).
- Pet care products for SA climates.
- Baby and toddler gear.

### Niche selection checklist

- [ ] 1,000+ monthly searches for the main keyword.
- [ ] 50+ long-tail keywords you can rank for.
- [ ] At least 3 high-ticket products to promote.
- [ ] Reasonable competition (top 10 sites not all major brands).

## Content Strategy for Affiliate Sites

Affiliate content is not the same as blog content. Every page must have a purpose.

### The Four Content Types

#### 1. Buyer's Guides (Highest Conversion)

"Best [product] for [use case]" — these convert the highest because readers are ready to buy.

Example: *Best Portable Power Stations for Load Shedding in 2026*.

#### 2. Product Reviews (Highest Trust)

Single-product deep reviews. Rank for "[product name] review".

Example: *EcoFlow Delta 2 Review — Tested Over 30 Days of Load Shedding*.

#### 3. Comparison Posts (High Intent)

"Product A vs Product B" — captures buyers in the final decision stage.

Example: *Jackery 1000 vs EcoFlow Delta 2 — Which Wins for SA Homes?*

#### 4. Informational Content (Top of Funnel)

"How to" articles. Lower conversion but they build authority and backlinks.

Example: *How to Calculate Your Solar Backup Needs During Load Shedding*.

### Content Production Schedule

- 2 buyer's guides per month.
- 4 product reviews per month.
- 2 comparison posts per month.
- 4 informational articles per month.

That is 12 articles per month — enough to build momentum in 6–9 months.

## SEO Basics for SA Affiliate Sites

SEO is how you get traffic without paying for ads.

### On-Page SEO Checklist

1. **Title tag** — include primary keyword + emotional hook.
2. **Meta description** — 155 characters, include keyword.
3. **H1** — matches the title.
4. **H2/H3 structure** — logical, keyword-rich subheadings.
5. **Image alt text** — descriptive, includes keywords.
6. **Internal linking** — link from informational posts to buyer's guides.
7. **External authority links** — link to Wikipedia, manufacturer pages.
8. **Page speed** — use Hostinger's built-in caching, compress images.

### Technical SEO Must-Dos

- Submit XML sitemap to Google Search Console.
- Use a clean URL structure (`/best-solar-power-stations/`).
- Mobile-first design (Google indexes mobile version first).
- Use schema markup for reviews (star ratings in search results).

### Backlink Strategy

- Guest post on SA tech blogs.
- Get featured in local "best of" lists.
- HARO (Help A Reporter Out) responses.
- Build relationships with other niche site owners.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Picking a niche too broad.** "Best tech" will never rank.
2. **Writing for search engines, not humans.** Google now penalises low-quality content.
3. **No internal linking.** This is the #1 missed SEO win.
4. **Promoting too many products.** Confused readers don't buy.
5. **Quitting at month 4.** Affiliate income is exponential, not linear.
6. **Ignoring the Hostinger affiliate program.** It often pays better than Amazon.
