## Why a 4K Monitor Matters

If you spend 8+ hours a day staring at a screen, the screen matters more than the chair (almost). Here is what 4K gives you:

- **Sharper text** — 4x the pixel density of 1080p. Less eye strain.
- **More workspace** — fit two full windows side-by-side without squinting.
- **Better colour accuracy** — essential for design, photo, and video work.
- **Future-proofing** — 4K is the new standard; 1080p is for budget setups.

### Specs That Actually Matter

#### Resolution
4K = 3840 x 2160. Anything below is not 4K. Period.

#### Panel Type
- **IPS** — best colour accuracy and viewing angles. The default choice.
- **VA** — best contrast, good for movies, weaker colours.
- **OLED** — best everything, but expensive. Avoid budget OLEDs.

#### Refresh Rate
- **60Hz** — fine for design, writing, coding.
- **120Hz+** — better for video editing, gaming, smooth UI.

#### Connectivity
Look for USB-C with Power Delivery (PD). One cable charges your laptop and sends video. Worth its weight in gold.

#### Size
- **27 inches** — the sweet spot for 4K. Pixel density is perfect.
- **32 inches** — great for design work, but requires deeper desk.
- **24 inches** — too small for 4K. Pixels wasted.

## Dual-Monitor Setups: Why Two Screens Beat One

A single 4K monitor is great. Two monitors is a productivity superpower.

### Why Dual Monitors Win

- **Reference + Working** — keep specs/video/design on one, work on the other.
- **Communication + Work** — Slack/email on one, your work on the other.
- **Code + Preview** — VS Code on one, browser preview on the other.
- **Less alt-tabbing** — studies show dual monitors save 2+ hours per week.

## Monitor Arms: The Upgrade Nobody Regrets

Every monitor in this guide supports VESA mounting. Ditch the included stand and use a monitor arm instead. Benefits:

- **Reclaims desk space** — your keyboard slides under the monitor.
- **Perfect ergonomics** — adjust height, angle, distance freely.
- **Cleaner look** — cable management through the arm.
- **Multi-monitor made easy** — a dual arm holds both screens in perfect alignment.

A monitor arm costs R800 — R2,500 on [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H99MHP7T?tag=freedomwheels-20) and is the single highest-rated upgrade in every freelancer's setup.

## Common Monitor Buying Mistakes

1. **Going too small.** 24-inch 4K wastes the resolution.
2. **Skipping USB-C.** It is the cable you didn't know you needed.
3. **Choosing VA for design work.** Colours are weaker than IPS.
4. **Forgetting the stand/arm.** VESA mount saves your neck.
5. **Buying one when you need two.** Dual monitors are a productivity cheat code.
6. **No calibration.** Even cheap monitors benefit from a basic colour calibration.
