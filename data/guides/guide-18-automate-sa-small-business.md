## Step-by-Step Automation Setup

### Step 1: Audit Your Repetitive Tasks (Week 1)

For one week, write down every task you do more than once. Look for:

- Same WhatsApp message sent daily.
- Same invoice format created weekly.
- Same social post template used.
- Same follow-up email for unpaid invoices.

These are automation candidates.

### Step 2: Set Up ConvertKit (Week 2)

1. Create a free [ConvertKit](https://convertkit.com/affiliates?ref=freedomwheels) account.
2. Build a welcome sequence (5 emails).
3. Add a form to your website.
4. Tag subscribers based on what they signed up for.

### Step 3: Build Your Canva System (Week 2)

1. Set up your [Canva Pro](https://www.canva.com/affiliates?ref=freedomwheels) Brand Kit.
2. Create 5 master templates (quote, sale, blog, testimonial, story).
3. Use Bulk Create to generate a month of content from a spreadsheet.

### Step 4: Add Zapier or Make.com (Week 3)

Zapier connects tools that don't natively talk to each other. Examples:

- New WhatsApp lead → adds to ConvertKit → sends welcome email.
- New Stripe payment → creates a Slack alert → updates a Google Sheet.
- New Canva design → auto-posts to Instagram at scheduled time.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Automating bad processes.** Fix the workflow first, then automate it.
2. **Over-automating.** Some touchpoints (complaints, big deals) need a human.
3. **No documentation.** If your VA can't understand your automations, you have a single point of failure.
4. **Ignoring analytics.** ConvertKit shows open rates — act on them.
5. **Buying too many tools.** Three good tools beat ten half-used ones.
