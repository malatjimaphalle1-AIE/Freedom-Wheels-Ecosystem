Head to [Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates?ref=freedomwheels) and grab the Business web hosting plan. Add your `.co.za` domain at checkout. Total cost: under R1,500 for 12 months.

### Step 2: Pick a Service Template (Day 1)

Hostinger has dozens of service-business templates. Pick one with:
- A clear hero section with a "Get a Quote" button.
- A services page (one page per service you offer).
- A testimonials block.
- A contact form with WhatsApp click-to-chat.

### Step 3: Write Your Pages (Day 2–3)

You need five pages minimum:
1. **Home** — what you do, who you serve, one strong call-to-action.
2. **Services** — list every service with a price range (transparency wins).
3. **About** — your story, qualifications, area covered.
4. **Reviews / Testimonials** — real quotes from real clients.
5. **Contact** — form, WhatsApp, phone, and map.

### Step 4: Set Up Google Business Profile (Day 4)

This is the most underused growth lever for SA service businesses. Go to `business.google.com`, claim your business, and complete every field:
- Upload 10+ photos of real work.
- List all your services.
- Get 5 happy customers to leave reviews in week one.
- Post weekly updates (Google rewards activity).

### Step 5: Add Online Booking (Day 5)

Embed a Calendly or SimplyBook.me widget so clients can pick a time slot and pay a deposit. This filters out tyre-kickers and cuts your admin time by 60%.

## Google Business Profile: The Hidden Goldmine

A fully optimised Google Business Profile will get you more leads than your website in months 1–3. Here is the playbook:

1. **Verify immediately** — Google mails you a postcard with a code.
2. **Pick the right categories** — "Plumber" primary, "Emergency Plumber" secondary.
3. **Write a keyword-rich description** — mention every suburb you serve.
4. **Post weekly** — Google posts rank in local search.
5. **Reply to every review** — yes, even the 5-star ones.

A tradesperson who does this in Cape Town can show up in the local pack within 4–6 weeks. That is free traffic worth thousands of rand per month.

## Common Mistakes Local Owners Make

- **Building a website and never updating it.** A stale site hurts more than no site.
- **Ignoring Google Business Profile.** This is the #1 lead source for local trades.
- **Not collecting reviews.** Ask every happy customer — within 24 hours.
- **No WhatsApp option.** SA customers prefer WhatsApp over phone calls.
- **Listing prices as "POA."** Transparent pricing wins trust and qualifies leads.
