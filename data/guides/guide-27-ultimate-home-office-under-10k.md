## 1. The Desk (R2,000)

Your desk is the foundation. Get this wrong and everything else suffers.

### What to Look For
- **Width:** Minimum 120cm. 140–160cm is ideal for dual monitors.
- **Depth:** Minimum 60cm. Less and your monitor is too close.
- **Height:** 73–75cm standard. Consider a standing desk converter if you cannot stand-sit easily.
- **Surface:** Matte finish to reduce glare. Avoid glass.

## Common Setup Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Spending more on the desk than the chair.** Your back disagrees.
2. **Monitor too low.** Top of screen should be at eye level.
3. **Window behind you on calls.** Always face the window, never have it behind you.
4. **Cluttered desk.** Empty space = head space.
5. **Noisy chair.** Test armrests squeak before buying.
6. **Cables everywhere.** R200 fixes this forever.
