## The Inventory Funding Challenge in SA E-commerce

Here is the dirty secret of e-commerce: growth costs money. If you sell R100,000 of product this month, you need R60,000 — R80,000 of cash to restock for next month — and that is before Black Friday demands 4x normal inventory. Most SA e-commerce founders face three ugly options:

1. **Under-stock.** Run out of bestsellers on Black Friday weekend and lose 40% of revenue.
2. **Personal credit cards.** 24% interest compounds fast.
3. **Traditional bank loans.** 6 weeks of paperwork, mountains of financials, often declined.

There is a fourth option: alternative SME finance.

## Designing Unboxing Inserts with Canva

The unboxing insert is where you convert a one-time buyer into a repeat customer. Bad inserts get thrown away. Great inserts drive social shares, repeat purchases, and reviews.

[Canva](https://www.canva.com/affiliates?ref=freedomwheels) is the easiest way to design professional inserts without a graphic designer.

### Types of Inserts to Design in Canva

1. **Thank-you card** — handwritten feel with a discount code.
2. **Care instructions** — relevant for apparel, electronics, beauty.
3. **Referral card** — "Give R50, get R50."
4. **Social share card** — "Tag us @yourbrand for a feature."
5. **Product guide** — cross-sell related products.
6. **Brand story card** — connect emotionally.

### Canva Workflow for Inserts

1. Set up your [Canva Pro](https://www.canva.com/affiliates?ref=freedomwheels) Brand Kit (colours, fonts, logo).
2. Choose an insert template sized to your envelope or box.
3. Customise the copy to your brand voice.
4. Export as print-ready PDF (300 DPI).
5. Send to a SA printer (Printulu, Cape Press, Online Printers).
6. Bulk-print 500–2,000 inserts.

### What Every Insert Must Include

- A clear single CTA (discount code, review request, social tag).
- Your logo and brand colour.
- A scannable QR code linking to a relevant page.
- Your social handles.
- A short personal-feel message.

## Common E-commerce Funding Mistakes

1. **Waiting until November to apply for funding.** Lulalend takes 48 hours, but order lead times are 4–6 weeks.
2. **Under-ordering bestsellers.** Stock-outs during Black Friday cost more than excess inventory.
3. **No unboxing strategy.** Generic packaging = no repeat customers.
4. **DIY insert design.** A Canva template looks better than Microsoft Word.
5. **Forgetting the post-purchase email.** The first email after delivery drives reviews.
