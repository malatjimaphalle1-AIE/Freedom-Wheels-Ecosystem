## Pricing for Corporate Clients

Stop charging by the hour. Corporate clients pay for outcomes.

### Hourly (Avoid)
"I charge R500/hour." — Penalises efficiency, caps your income.

### Project-Based (Good)
"This engagement is R85,000 + VAT for the full deliverable." — Predictable, profitable.

### Retainer (Best)
"R25,000/month for ongoing X, Y, Z." — Recurring revenue, predictable cash flow.

### Value-Based (Premium)
"R150,000 — based on the R1.2M revenue this work will generate." — Highest rates, hardest to justify.

For SA corporate clients, project-based with optional retainer add-ons is the sweet spot. Always include VAT (if registered) and quote in ZAR for local clients, USD for international.

## Contracts: Don't Skip This

Corporate clients expect a contract. Without one, you are an amateur.

Essential contract clauses:

1. **Scope of work** — be specific.
2. **Payment terms** — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery is standard.
3. **Late payment penalty** — 2% per month.
4. **Kill fee** — 25% if they cancel mid-project.
5. **IP transfer** — only after final payment.
6. **Limitation of liability** — cap at the project value.
7. **Confidentiality** — mutual NDA.
8. **Termination** — 30 days written notice.

Use a SA-specific freelancer contract template (Lawyerspot, Legalwise, or have one drafted for R3,000 — R8,000). It will pay for itself the first time a client tries to scope-creep you.
