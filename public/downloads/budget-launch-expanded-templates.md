## Template 4: Email Marketing Sequence (5 Emails)

A 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers or customers.

### Email 1: Welcome + Deliver
**Subject:** Welcome to [brand name] — here's your [free resource/product]
**Goal:** Deliver what you promised + set expectations
**Body:**
Hi [Name],
Welcome! Here's [what they signed up for]: [link]
Over the next few days, I'll share [what to expect — e.g., "3 tips on launching your first online business in SA"].
Talk soon,
[Your name]

### Email 2: Story + Value
**Subject:** The mistake that cost me R5,000 (and how you can avoid it)
**Goal:** Build connection through story + deliver value
**Body:**
Hi [Name],
When I started, I [story about a mistake — e.g., "bought R5,000 worth of tools before talking to a single customer"].
The lesson: [what you learned — e.g., "validate before you build"].
Here's how you can avoid the same mistake: [actionable tip].
[Your name]

### Email 3: Educational
**Subject:** The one formula that changed how I price my services
**Goal:** Teach something valuable that builds trust
**Body:**
Hi [Name],
[Teach a useful concept — e.g., the offer formula from the guide]
[Provide an example]
[Tell them to try it themselves]
[Your name]

### Email 4: Soft Pitch
**Subject:** A resource that might help you
**Goal:** Introduce your product/service without hard selling
**Body:**
Hi [Name],
I created [product name] because [why you made it — e.g., "I kept seeing the same mistakes in SA Facebook groups"].
It includes [3 key benefits].
If that sounds useful, you can check it out here: [link]
No pressure — the free content will keep coming regardless.
[Your name]

### Email 5: Urgency + Final Value
**Subject:** Last tip from me (for now)
**Goal:** Deliver final value + gentle reminder about your product
**Body:**
Hi [Name],
Here's my last tip for this series: [final actionable tip].
If you found this helpful, [product name] goes much deeper: [link].
Whatever you decide, I'm rooting for you.
[Your name]

## Template 8: Price Testing Framework

A systematic approach to finding the right price for your product.

### Step 1: Set your initial price
Based on:
- Competitor pricing research
- Your cost break-even
- Your perception of value
- SA market willingness to pay

**Initial test price:** R_____

### Step 2: Define your test parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Test duration | e.g., 14 days |
| Traffic source | e.g., Facebook group post, LinkedIn |
| Expected visitors | e.g., 100-200 |
| Expected conversion rate | 2-5% (industry average for digital products) |
| Expected sales | 2-10 |

### Step 5: Compare results

| Metric | Price A (R___) | Price B (R___) | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total revenue | R_____ | R_____ | _____ |
| Conversion rate | _____% | _____% | _____ |
| Revenue per visitor | R_____ | R_____ | _____ |
| Refund rate | _____% | _____% | _____ |
| Customer satisfaction | _____/5 | _____/5 | _____ |

### Step 6: Decision framework

- **If higher price has similar conversion:** Use the higher price
- **If lower price has much higher conversion but lower total revenue:** Use the higher price
- **If lower price has much higher conversion AND higher total revenue:** Use the lower price
- **If refund rate is >10% at either price:** Improve the product before lowering price

**Important:** Don't change prices based on fewer than 10 sales. Small samples are misleading.
